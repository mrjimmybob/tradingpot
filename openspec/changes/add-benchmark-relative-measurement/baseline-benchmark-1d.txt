A zero row therefore does NOT mean the strategy was inactive, and expectancy is simply not the right instrument for it: read the '> B&H' and '> DCA' columns and that strategy's benchmark-relative section instead.
'> B&H' / '> DCA' count the windows in which the strategy's return exceeded that benchmark's - a count, not a score. 'Expo' is mean realised exposure (beta to the asset): a strategy well below 1.00 was less exposed than buy-and-hold, so part of any shortfall against B&H is exposure rather than selection, and the DCA column is the fairer comparison for it.
